What is this?

This is an unofficial, educational knowledge base for MITRE ATT&CK®. Every technique guide is explained in-depth — simulation, red-team view, blue-team view, detection engineering and a real-world case study — and every Sigma rule is validated by a CI gate with positive and negative tests before it ships. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by The MITRE Corporation.
